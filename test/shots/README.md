# Shots: the headless check rig

Run `npm install` here once, then `node test/shots/run.mjs [--base URL] [--pages home coaching] [--jobs 4]` from the repo root. Without `--base` it serves the repo root with vercel.json's headers and a stub for `/api/*`. It drives the installed Chrome (`CHROME_PATH` to change), because Playwright's Chromium cannot play H.264.

It opens every page in `site/site.js` in light and dark (stored `chama-theme`, with the system set to the opposite), full and low motion (`chama-motion`), at 364x610 with a 17px font and at 1440x900. Full motion is shot at the top, the middle of each world's hold and the end; low motion as one full page. Extras: no JavaScript on `/` and `/engineering-department`, 610x364 one column on every page, a dark router trip from `/` to `/engineering-department` and Back, and `/#thanks`.

Every state must have: no horizontal overflow, console errors, failed or off-origin requests; no visible text under the bare header (checked every half screen); a first frame in the stored theme; a sky that reads as its theme (never black in light); AA contrast for every visible text node against its world's `--ground` or the nearest painted background; a hold on every pinned world; in light full motion at both sizes, no dead scroll (more than a quarter screen, checked every tenth, where under 12% of the screen shows anything at 35% opacity or more); no hidden words in low motion or without JavaScript; in full motion, every word that stands as its pin leaves (`data-stand`) in the same place, less its exit lift, at the start and the middle of its stretch, checked after the page has been scrolled around. On `/` and `/engineering-department` the H1 is the LCP element, and on `/` at 364x610 the three doors are in view after one screen of scroll.

**Frame fill** (Elliot, 2026-11-16: no awkward empty space, every frame cozy). In light full motion at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 364x610 (17px), every eighth of a screen, it measures how much of the screen's height the visible words and figures span together. A run of more than a quarter screen under the floor (40% on desktop, 50% on the phone) is a floor miss, named by the world under the middle of the screen. At 60% of each pinned world's hold it also measures the targets in `docs/DESIGN.md` (the frame): the group (55% desktop, 65% phone), the figure (45%, or 30% tall and 90% of the column wide on the phone), the distance between the centres of words and figure (8% on desktop) and the largest empty band (35%, 30%). A floor miss fails the run only in a world marked `data-fill`; everywhere else, and for every target, it is reported in `report.json` under `fill` and counted in the last lines of the output, with a shot of the emptiest frame of each floor miss (`fill-<size>-floor-<y>.png`). Mark a world `data-fill` once it meets the floor, so it cannot slip back. A world that cannot meet it goes in `FILL_EXEMPT` in `run.mjs`, by selector, with the reason.

Output goes to `test/shots/out/` (ignored): the shots per page, `sheet-<page>.png` contact sheets with failing shots outlined in red, and `report.json` listing each failure with its page, state and shot, and the fill report. The exit code is 1 when anything fails.
- Text under the header is checked every half screen, which can miss a line that rides under it between samples. After a layout change, also probe every eighth of a screen at 364x610, 375x548, 390x664, 1280x720 and 1440x900 (2026-11-16: the loop's closing line passed the rig and failed the finer probe in production).
