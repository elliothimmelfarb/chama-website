Why it bounced

Before

The page held the words

  1. Your finger moves the page, and the phone draws it right away.
  2. The words move up with it.
  3. A frame or two later the page's script catches up and pushes them back down.
  4. Result: up, snap down, up, snap down. That's the bounce, and the catch you felt as each section released.
After

The browser holds them

  1. The page tells the browser once: hold these words still for this stretch of scroll.
  2. The browser applies it in the same step that moves the page.
  3. The words never move with the page, so there is nothing to snap back.
  4. Older browsers without this feature keep the previous behaviour.
