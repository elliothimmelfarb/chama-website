On the live site

What changed on the Words pages

  • RemovedThe Pictures view: the switch, view.js, its CSS, and the three lines that existed only for it.
  • ChangedThe switch is now two links, Words and Worlds, so each version has its own address and works without JavaScript.
  • AddedMoving between pages dissolves instead of flashing, in browsers that support cross-page transitions.

Rails

  • HeldNo new claims. The only new copy is the label "Worlds" and its tooltip, recorded in the brain before release.
  • HeldZero external requests on all eight public pages, now a test.
  • HeldWorlds pages point search engines at their Words page and stay out of the sitemap.

Checks

  • Pass417 tests, 17 of them new and written before the pages existed.
  • PassLive: all six addresses load, no console errors, no outside requests, the flame room opens on its question, and the switch goes to /worlds/about and back.
  • PassLaptop and phone layouts, with no sideways scroll at 390 pixels wide.

Worth knowing

  • DeployShipped from a clean export of main, because the main checkout is on another session's branch, hearth-stripe-checkout. Deploying from that folder would have shipped that branch.
  • UntestedNot yet run on a real phone, only in headless Chrome at phone size.
  • Old links?view=pictures links now open the Words page.
