The ones that mattered

  1. highFree session credits. Two concurrent cancels of one booking each minted a refund. Both cancel paths are now compare-and-set on the row's status. Same fix on referral rewards.
  2. highSelf-promotion. Anyone delegated members.manage could set their own role to staff. Refused now, and roles are granted by rank, not by permission set.
  3. highWatchdog fence escape. Visitor text was pasted raw into the reviewer prompt, so a closing tag in a chat message could steer the verdict, including to a 24-hour shutdown. Turn content is now encoded before it reaches the reviewer.
  4. highRetention promise was false. The privacy page says conversations are deleted within 12 months; the cleanup only ever swept intake notes. It sweeps chat transcripts too now, with tests.
  5. highInternal reports on the public domain. The Hearth pentest and prompt audit were live at /docs/reports/... with no noindex. docs/ and evals/ are excluded from deploys, plus a noindex header and a robots rule.
  6. highErased reply. After a note was sent to Elliot, the agent's last words vanished from the visitor's screen. One-line painter fix.
  7. highWrong timezone on the owner's calendar. The lede named the viewer's zone over rows drawn in the owner's. Fixed, and the time-off form now says which zone it is in.

What the reviewers caught in the fixes

Twelve problems, all fixed before merge. The two that would have hurt: dropping tools on the final model call would have made that call an API 400 every time (now tool_choice: none instead), and the new feed headers would have noindexed the public feed (removed). Also: the owner had been locked out of stepping down, role checks compared permission sets instead of rank, a referral could be marked rewarded and never paid, and an orphaned flame worker in one failure path.

Deferred, deliberately

  • openRate limiting on /api/chat. A curl loop with no Origin header can spend model calls without limit. Needs a token bucket keyed on address, probably reusing the Hearth limiter. Design work, not a patch.
  • openTranscript blob overwrite. A conversation is one blob keyed by the client's id, so an attacker can shrink their own transcript before the watchdog reads it. Needs a per-turn key or an append-only record.
  • openAgent mount/destroy lifecycle (global listeners are never removed; harmless today with one mount per page).
  • openLive-region churn during animated text for screen readers, and skeleton tiles on the Hearth home.
