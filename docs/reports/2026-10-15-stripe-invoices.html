Worth knowing

  1. The Stripe invoice is the payment request, not the fatura. That still comes from the certified software.
  2. Your Stripe account is shared with ShopIntake, and ShopIntake's webhook already sends a fatura notice for every paid invoice. Hearth payments will show up there too, which is what the accountant needs. Hearth invoices are tagged source: hearth.
  3. An invoice you send by hand from the Stripe dashboard also lands in the member's account, matched by their email. ShopIntake's subscription invoices never do.
  4. The email to the member comes from the Hearth, not Stripe, so it matches the other Hearth mail.
  5. Checkout, where people pay before booking, is not built yet, as you asked.
  6. Screens above are from a local copy with a stand-in Stripe. In production I checked the webhook (it rejects unsigned calls and accepts signed ones) and the new database table.
