How a contact request now reaches Elliot

  1. VisitorGives a name and a way to reach themEmail, or a number (asked once if it's WhatsApp)
  2. AgentShows exactly what will be sentThen asks "Send it?"
  3. VisitorSays yesA change inside the yes gets shown again
  4. CodeChecks the conversationDetails typed by the visitor and shown just before the yes; one per reply
  5. Inbox"Someone asked to be contacted"With the conversation id; visitor text can't forge lines
