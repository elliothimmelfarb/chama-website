How it is built

  • One source, no drift. The card is brand/og/og-card.html, plain HTML on the site's own tokens and font stack. brand/og/render.sh screenshots it with headless Chrome at 1200x630. Nothing is fetched from the network.
  • A dated filename. WhatsApp and other messengers cache a preview by URL and offer no way to force a refetch, so the image ships as og-2026-09-22.png. The old og.png stays in place for links already sent.
  • No new claim. Every line on the card restates copy already on the homepage. The wording is recorded in the brain's website page. A first deck, "then kept running and changing as you grow", was cut on Elliot's review.
  • Every page. Home, about, training, and privacy all point at the new image with the new alt text. Verified live with a fresh fetch after deploy.
