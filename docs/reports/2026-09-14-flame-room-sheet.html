Copy

Removed under the headline: the line "Say it plainly. We ask a question or two…" and the five starter chips ("Orders get retyped into three places." and the rest). The row of the same prompts that appeared above the composer mid-conversation went with them. The placeholder "Tell us what gets in your way" is the only prompt left. Same change on /agent, which shares the app.

What was wrong on phones

The room sat at the end of a long page with the transcript as a second scroller inside it. Four referees tried to decide which one a thumb meant: a document scroll snap at the room's bottom edge, a "seat" that opened the transcript only once the room sat flush, a hand-routed chain that scrolled the page from inside the transcript with preventDefault, and a keyboard pin that released on blur. Each one could move the page under a finger that had not asked for it, and iOS's URL bar collapse counted as a scroll to all of them.

The change, in code

PieceNow
cut scroll snapscroll-snap-type on html and the room-unsnapped arming logic, gone from site.css and agent.js.
cut seat on touchSeat detection, magnet and settle timers run only on pointer devices. The laptop wheel path is untouched.
cut chainingThe touchmove handler that scrolled the page from inside the transcript is gone.
new sheetEmbedded on touch, the pin (kb-pin) stays up for the whole conversation. html.room-pinned parks the document; a pull past the transcript's edge or a drag on the sheet's chrome is prevented so nothing behind moves.
new ways inFocus the composer, or tap a transcript already under way (no keyboard). Links and buttons inside it still do their own thing.
new way outBack to page unpins and returns the page to the exact scroll position it left.

Net: 123 lines added, 429 removed across agent.js, agent.css, site.css, README.

Verified

  • Headless Playwright, iPhone 13 emulation, against the live site: touch flicks carry the page through the room without the transcript catching; focus pins the sheet at the top of the screen; send and blur keep it; a flick on the sheet leaves the page's scroll position untouched; Back to page restores it; tapping the transcript reopens the sheet without raising the keyboard.
  • Desktop wheel seat and the /agent keyboard pin behave as before. No console errors.
  • Removed strings are absent from the deployed agent.js and site.css.

Worth a real thumb

The emulator cannot reproduce iOS's own keyboard animation or the URL bar collapse. The two moments to try on a phone: tap the composer and watch the sheet ride the keyboard in, and close the keyboard and scroll a long reply. If either stutters, the visual viewport tracking in writeViewport is where to look.
