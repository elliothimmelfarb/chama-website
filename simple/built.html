Made for one business

Toraya Lisboa

Toraya is a Japanese deli in Rossio station. We built their website, and an app for the staff that goes with it. They circle any part of the site, write what they want changed, and the change is live within the hour. The version they approved was ready four hours after we started, after three rounds of their feedback.

Made for one business

VSF Lead Automation

Vivir Sin Fronteras helps students get into universities abroad. VSF Lead Automation is the system we built to handle their leads. When someone enquires on the website, a bot messages them on WhatsApp, asks for consent, and then asks the questions the team would ask to work out whether a meeting is worth it. If it is, the bot books it straight into the team’s calendar. Anyone on the team can take over a conversation and hand it back, and a dashboard shows how the leads are doing.

This is private software, built for one business.

Made for one business

Alves Teamwear

Alves Teamwear sells custom kits to sports teams. Getting an order to the supplier used to take a lot of manual back and forth. We built an app where a team designs its own kit and places the order, and a command center behind it where the operators and the supplier keep track of everything. Most of the manual steps now happen on their own.

This is private software, built for one business.

Our own product

ShopIntake

ShopIntake fills the gap between the purchase order a customer emails you and your ERP. It reads the PDF and pulls the order out, so nobody has to type it in. It also flags anything that needs your attention, tells you when a part isn’t on your list, and makes a run plan across several POs.

Our own product

View the Place

View the Place makes 3D models of homes that are for sale or for rent. We build the model from the floor plan and photos, and buyers can walk through the home on their phone before they visit.

viewtheplace.com

Our own product

The Hearth

The Hearth is the platform we run our AI coaching on. Elliot sets his calendar, and members book their sessions and pay for them there. It keeps a record of every session, and it has roles and permissions for teams. It also has an MCP server, so members can connect their own AI agents to get the summaries of our calls, what we talked about, and the homework.

chamainteligente.com/hearth

Free to explore

Battle of Mogadishu

A 3D reconstruction of the Battle of Mogadishu, 3 and 4 October 1993, at full scale and in real time. It’s built from the historical record and not from the film. You can fly anywhere in the city, follow a helicopter, move through time, and open the sources behind every event. It’s meant as a memorial to everyone who died there.
