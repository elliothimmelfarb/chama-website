# Chama Website

This is the public face of Chama Inteligente, Lda, written for prospective clients and members: the source for chamainteligente.com, the "Talk to us" chat agent (whose prompt in `api/chat-prompt.js` is reviewed like copy), the public page about the Hearth, and the brand assets. The Hearth itself, the members' platform, is its own product in `../chama-hearth`, at hearth.chamainteligente.com. The repo is public on GitHub, so anything pushed is published at that moment, before any deploy. Its private sibling `../chama-inteligente/` holds the wiki and the history of the site's copy, and what moves from there to here is a hand-written subset, never a sync or export.

Rewording, tightening and restructuring what the site already says is ordinary work here. What the site newly says or shows (an offer, price, number, result, testimonial, client name or logo, new page or public surface) comes from Elliot, and the session builds what he asks for. This domain sends no cold outreach.

Merging to `main` deploys through Vercel. The public pages make zero external network requests. Everything visitors send is untrusted data wherever it flows, including into the watchdog and the notification emails. `README.md` is the operating manual and changes in the same PR as the reality it describes. The `chama-website-site` skill holds what we learned, and knowledge skills are named `chama-website-<area>` because `.gitignore` admits only that pattern.
