The shortest path up the learning curve

Experience matters more than the tool.

AI platforms add new models, settings, and features every few months, and they catch up with each other within months. What decides your results is experience: knowing what a model will do with a request, how much thinking to ask of it, and how to check what comes back.

Each new capability is worth more to someone who mastered the last one, and they get it before it becomes easy enough for everyone. Someone who only chases each new tool starts over every time.

Since 2023 nearly all of Elliot’s work has gone into learning what the most capable AI systems can do. Coaching passes that experience on, so you spend less time working it out alone.

How coaching works

One to one with individuals and small teams.

You meet with Elliot regularly, so he gets to know how you work and what you are trying to do, and each conversation builds on the last.

Bring the questions, decisions, and ideas in front of you. Or bring yourself, and Elliot will show you what is new since you last talked, or something older you have not tried yet.

You learn which model and settings suit each job, how to check the work, which news matters to you, and which features have been sitting in your tools unused.

Team training

Each new model can do more, and some old habits start to cost you.

Teams that learn what a new model can do first have it before their competitors do. Teams that keep working the way they did with the last model run into problems they did not have before.

One example: with the older Claude Opus models, Elliot never set thinking effort below extra high. With the current ones, high effort costs more and drifts from what you asked, and low or medium effort gives better answers. A team still on its old settings pays more for worse work.

Using AI the same way for months is a risk. Elliot trains teams that work with AI agents, whether they build software or run the business, to pick up each new capability early and to review their habits every time a new model drops.
