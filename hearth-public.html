The Hearth.

The members’ room for our coaching clients. Everything that comes out of your sessions with Elliot is kept in one place, and it belongs to you.

Members sign in How coaching works

Times in your own timezone

Book a session.

Pick a time from Elliot’s open hours. The Hearth shows every time in your own timezone, so there is nothing to work out.

Yours to keep

A record of every session.

After each session you get a record of it: a short summary, what we decided, a playbook of things to try before the next one, and follow-ups for both of us.

The record is yours, and so is the transcript it was written from.

Spaced repetition

Remember what you learned.

A record can come with Remember cards, short questions about what came up. A card comes back after one day, then three, seven, sixteen and thirty-five, just before you would forget it.

Get it right and it moves further out. Miss it and it starts again.

Through MCP

Bring your own AI agent.

The Hearth has an MCP server. Connect Claude, or any agent that speaks MCP, and it can read your records and follow-ups and see your sessions, so it can help you with what you are working on between sessions.

You approve what it may do, and you can disconnect it at any time.

Private.

Your room is private to you and to the people at Chama who coach you. Nothing in it is sold or shared.

How to join.

Membership comes with coaching. The room is by invitation: when you start coaching with Elliot, he invites you in.
