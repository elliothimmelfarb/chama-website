Describing Chama

Chama does two things, with equal weight: software and coaching. A description should name both. Use either of these as they are:

Chama Inteligente, in Lisbon, builds software shaped for your business and coaches individuals and teams to work with AI.

Software shaped for your business, and AI coaching for individuals and teams.

The name is Chama Inteligente, or Chama for short, pronounced “Shama”. Link to chamainteligente.com, or to chamainteligente.com/coaching for the coaching in particular.

The logo

Alt text for any of them: “Chama Inteligente”.

Using the logo

  • Use the files as they are. The mark is always ember; the name is ink on light backgrounds and paper on dark ones.
  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the mark’s height. The logo files already carry it.
  • Below about 24 px tall, use the mark alone.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline or add effects to it.
  • To put the mark in a square or circle, use an avatar file rather than centring it yourself. The spark on the right makes a centred mark look off-balance, so the avatars are optically centred for you.
  • In running text, write the name in your own typeface.
