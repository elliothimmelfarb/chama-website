USING THE LOGO

- Use the files as they are. The mark is always ember; the name is ink on
  light backgrounds and paper on dark ones.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the mark's height.
- Below about 24 px tall, use the mark alone.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline or add effects to it.
- To put the mark in a square or circle, use an avatar file rather than
  centring it yourself. The spark on the right makes a centred mark look
  off-balance, so the avatars are optically centred for you.
- In running text, write the name in your own typeface.
