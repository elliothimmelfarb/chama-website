What makes a good fit

  • You believe AI changes what is possible.
    The underside

    Coaching only works for someone who already believes there is something here worth going after. No coach can supply that belief. Being worn out by the pace does not rule you out; the pace wears everyone out some days.

  • You want to learn how to use that power.
    The underside

    Learning is the point of the work. If you would rather hand a problem to someone and get an answer back, you want a vendor, and good vendors exist. What I teach is how to do it yourself: we build real things together, and what you learn keeps working after we are done.

  • You want to find the signal in the noise of AI news and headlines.
    The underside

    Any list of answers I hand you would be stale within months, because the tools keep changing. What lasts is knowing how to tell what matters for your own situation, and that is the skill this work builds.
