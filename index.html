What we offer

The shortest path up the learning curve

AI changes more than the tools we use.

It changes how we think, work, organize, and lead.

Most AI courses teach you how to use today’s tools and capabilities for a handful of tasks.

We teach what lasts: the ideas and habits that help you explore your environment for new capabilities that may not have been available yesterday.

So you grow with AI instead of chasing it.

The shape of the work

One to one with individuals and small teams.

You meet with Elliot regularly, so he can get to know how you work, keep track of what you are trying to do, and make each conversation build on the last.

Bring the questions, decisions, and ideas in front of you. Or just bring yourself, and Elliot will teach you what is new since the last time you talked, or something old you have not learned yet.

Team training

We train engineering teams.

Elliot trains engineering teams in agentic engineering: building software with AI agents that plan, write, and verify code.

The capabilities change every few months. Most teams learn a workflow once and let it age. We teach the habits that keep a team evolving with the capabilities, so you are always building the way today’s tools allow, not the way last year’s did.

Software

Your software is your culture.

The software your company runs on decides what you can and cannot do. It gives you the words you use for your work. It sets the process everyone follows, whether anyone chose it or not.

Most companies rent that culture off the shelf and bend themselves to fit it.

Decide how you want to work instead. Then run on software that works that way.

Owning it

Software that only your company uses.

Owning your software used to mean hiring an engineering team and carrying it for years. AI-native development has changed the cost of both. Software shaped to one company is now something that company can afford to own.

We build it with you and maintain it. It bakes in how you work: your vocabulary, your process, your rules.

When you want to work differently, tell Elliot how. The software changes with you.

Prefer to build it yourselves? We also build alongside individuals and small teams, and teach them to develop software the AI-native way.
