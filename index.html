The shortest path up the learning curve

AI changes more than the tools we use.

It changes how we think, work, organize, and lead.

Most AI courses teach you how to use today’s tools and capabilities for a handful of tasks. I teach what lasts: the ideas and habits that help you explore your environment for new capabilities that may not have been available yesterday.

So you can stay ahead of what most people know how to do.
