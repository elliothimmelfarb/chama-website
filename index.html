01 / Judgment

This is not a tool problem.

AI changes too quickly for a fixed list of answers. The useful question is what matters for your situation now, what can wait, and how to turn one good idea into capability you keep.

The goal is not to make you dependent on a tool, a system, or a consultant. It is to help you combine your own experience, ideas, and strengths with new capabilities, so you can build, improve, and lead with them.

02 / The work

One direction. Three ways to start.

  1. 01 / One to one

    Private coaching

    Regular one-to-one work for a leader or professional who wants clearer judgment, practical capability, and a trusted guide through a fast-moving field. Sessions begin with your real work and build skills that compound over time.

  2. 02 / Together

    Leaders and teams

    Practical capability-building for leaders and small teams who want AI use to last. People combine their domain knowledge with new tools, then learn to evaluate opportunities, build useful ways of working, and improve them independently.

  3. 03 / Selective

    Very small companies

    Selective hands-on automation support for very small companies. This is a close extension of the coaching work, not a black-box system handed over without understanding.

03 / Agency

Built to disappear.

You know your customers, your work, and your constraints in ways I never will. My job is to help you add new fluency to that knowledge, use it in ways that matter, and then step back.

The best outcome is that you can lead this without me.
