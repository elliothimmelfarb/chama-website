AI coaching for business leaders

Everyone is training their workforce on AI. The ones pulling ahead are starting with leadership.

Companies are investing in AI training for their teams. That is a good instinct. Most of it fades within weeks, because when people go back to their desks, the person setting their priorities does not use AI, does not understand it, and cannot tell the difference between good work and a good demo.

When leaders go first, everything changes. Teams see what confident AI use looks like from the people they report to. Training sticks, because someone at the top reinforces it daily. The whole organization develops real momentum.

What I do

I am an AI coach.

I work with leaders directly, over time, building the mindset, the skills and the instincts that let them lead this transition from the front. Each session builds on the last. The skills compound. Over months you develop the kind of fluency that reshapes how your whole organization moves.

01

Coach leaders, one to one

We meet regularly. You bring the real questions your week generated. I help you build a way of thinking about AI that stays with you: how to judge what you are seeing, where it fits into your decisions, how to tell what matters from what is just loud.

02

Teach teams and run classes

Live sessions for a team once its leadership is fluent, and standalone classes on two subjects I can teach from my own working practice: building software with Claude Code without being a software engineer, and running a small autonomous organization that does routine work and reports to you.

I build AI platform software for a US software company on a continuing basis. What I teach on Monday is what I was doing on Friday. Sometimes the right answer to a problem is a coach, and sometimes it is a thing that gets built. I will say which one I think it is.

How it works

Four things, in order.

We start with a conversation

You tell me what you are working on and what you are trying to figure out. I tell you honestly whether I think I can help and what that would look like.

Then we meet regularly

The shape depends on you: a leader alone, a leader and their team, or a class. Sessions build on each other, which is the whole point and the reason a single event, however good, can only take you so far.

I only work with a small number of clients at a time

That is by design. You get a person who knows your name, remembers what you are working on, and picks up exactly where we left off.

Then I get out of the way

Your people have the domain expertise. They know your customers, your operations and your constraints in ways I never will. What they need is the fluency to apply that expertise in new ways. My job is to build it as fast and as deeply as I can, and then step back.

What shapes everything

Three ideas I keep coming back to.

Leaders first, always

When your leaders go first, everything downstream accelerates. Training sticks because someone at the top reinforces it. Adoption gains momentum because people can see what fluent AI use looks like from the person setting their priorities. The whole organization starts moving together, with direction, instead of in scattered pockets.

Skills that compound

Each session connects to what came before and sets up what comes next. I teach mental models and practices that build on each other, so that over weeks and months you develop instincts that let you move faster, see further and make better calls about where AI fits.

Built to disappear

The best outcome for both of us is the day your team can lead this entirely on their own. Everything I do is designed to bring that day closer.
